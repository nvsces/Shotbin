Shotbin sorts out the screenshots piling up in your photo library.

You take a screenshot to keep a code from a text message, a receipt, a restaurant address or a film someone recommended. A week later it is buried among a thousand other images and finding it is hopeless.

Shotbin reads the text on your screenshots directly on your device and sorts them onto shelves: codes and passwords, receipts and payments, tickets and bookings, addresses, contacts, links, conversations, recipes. Everything it finds becomes an action: copy a code with one tap, call a number, open an address on the map, add a date to your calendar.

WHAT IT DOES

• Shelves. Your screenshots are sorted by meaning, so you never scroll the library again.
• Reminders. A ticket for tomorrow or a booking on Friday will not slip away.
• Search inside images. Find a screenshot by a word you saw on the screen.
• Cleanup. Once a screenshot has done its job, delete the image and keep what it contained.
• Duplicates. Finds series of near-identical frames and suggests keeping the best one.
• Photos. Optionally checks your ordinary photos for duplicates, in a separate section.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Text recognition runs offline, using the system frameworks. No screenshot and no line of text is ever sent anywhere. No account, no cloud, no analytics.

The app asks for photo access to read your screenshots and delete the ones you choose. Calendar access is write-only and used solely to add events: Shotbin cannot see your existing appointments.
